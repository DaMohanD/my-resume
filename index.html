About

What I do and where I'm heading.

Seven years running data and operations inside container logistics has given me something most analysts have to simulate: messy, multi-source, high-stakes data and the stakeholders who depend on getting it right. I've used that ground to build end-to-end ML work — EDA and feature engineering through neural network training, model interpretability, containerised deployment and CI/CD. Two of my applications run in live depot operations today, and analysis I led improved depot uptime by roughly 15%. I'm completing an M.Sc. in Computer Science (AI & ML) at Woolf University through Scaler Neovarsity, and I'm looking for Data Scientist and ML Engineer roles where the modelling has to survive contact with real operations.

Projects

Two products running in production, plus case studies from my M.Sc. coursework.

Shipped products

  • Led end-to-end development of a full-stack platform automating container lifecycle tracking — intake, storage, repair, release — replacing spreadsheet-driven tracking with a rules-based automation layer that cut repetitive data entry and reconciliation work.
  • Built an embedded analytics layer surfacing throughput, dwell and exception patterns to depot teams in real time, removing the manual report-generation step from daily decisions.
  • Directed containerisation and deployment: Docker images to DockerHub, Flask APIs on AWS ECS, and GitHub Actions CI/CD automating build, test and release.
  • Led development of a production-grade material management system positioned as a lightweight, automation-first alternative to SAP MM for SME logistics companies, covering inventory tracking, purchase orders, GRN and reporting.
  • Built automated data-processing logic flagging stock anomalies, reorder thresholds and reconciliation mismatches, reducing manual review effort across inventory and purchase-order workflows.
  • Ran competitive analysis against ERPNext, Odoo and Zoho Inventory to find the automation and analytics gaps in existing SME tools, then positioned Matriq's reporting for the Indian SME logistics market with non-technical users in mind.

ML case studies

  • Framed delivery-time estimation as an ETA optimisation problem and built a neural network regression model predicting delivery time from order, item and store-level features.
  • Designed and trained a multilayer perceptron in TensorFlow/Keras, applying forward and backpropagation fundamentals, and benchmarked SGD, RMSProp and Adam for convergence and validation error.
  • Engineered features from real delivery data — distance, order value, staffing, traffic-adjacent signals — and applied regularisation and dropout to control overfitting.
  • Used LIME to explain individual delivery-time predictions, turning model behaviour into something a business stakeholder can act on.
  • Implemented a convolutional neural network from first principles in NumPy — convolution, pooling and backpropagation through conv layers — to understand the internals, then reproduced and extended the architecture in Keras for benchmarking.
  • Explored autoencoders for unsupervised feature learning and dimensionality reduction, comparing reconstruction quality and latent-space behaviour against a PCA baseline.
  • Built end-to-end ML pipelines on AWS SageMaker covering model training, evaluation and deployment.
  • Processed large-scale datasets with Apache Spark for distributed feature engineering and data transformation.
  • Built an end-to-end ML-powered tool in Streamlit for the Cars24 use case.
  • Integrated MLflow for experiment tracking, model versioning and data management across iterative development cycles.
  • Built an ensemble classifier (Random Forest + XGBoost) predicting driver churn from behavioural and transactional features.
  • Ran hyperparameter tuning and cross-validation, then used SHAP feature importance to identify the top churn drivers and interpret model output for business stakeholders.
  • Engineered 30+ features from raw delivery data — time-based, route-based and categorical encodings — to improve downstream model performance.
  • Applied outlier treatment, missing-value imputation and scaling pipelines, documenting the rationale for each feature so the work stays reproducible and explainable.
  • Designed a hybrid recommendation engine for a streaming catalogue, combining collaborative filtering with content-based NLP.
  • Evaluated with RMSE and precision@k, comparing cold-start behaviour across filtering strategies.
